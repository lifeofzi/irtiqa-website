# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Music fans and press discovering Ali Saffudin — primarily on mobile, often landing from Spotify, Instagram, or a link shared after hearing a track. Secondary: journalists and tastemakers doing a quick background check. Tertiary: merch buyers who've already decided they want something and need a clean checkout.

## Product Purpose

alisaffudin.com is the canonical home for Ali Saffudin's music, story, and merchandise. It exists to deepen connection after a first listen — move someone from curious to committed. Success looks like: streams go up, press contact form gets used, and merch sells without friction.

## Brand Personality

Raw, defiant, intimate. The site should feel like the music sounds: Kashmiri folk meets punk-rock grit. Not polished industry PR — something with texture and weight. The CRT scanlines, deep reds, and monospace type are doing real work: they communicate that this is not a manufactured pop product.

## Anti-references

- Generic Spotify artist microsites (white background, centered logo, streaming buttons in a row)
- Bandcamp default (functional but blank)
- Over-produced major-label artist sites (too glossy, too corporate)
- The warm-neutral "editorial indie" aesthetic (cream/sand tones, clean sans-serifs — reads as coffee shop playlist, not Kashmir punk)

## Design Principles

1. **Texture over polish** — intentional roughness (scanlines, deep blacks, monospace) beats smooth and generic. Every element should feel like it came from the same world as the music.
2. **The image is the message** — photography and video are the strongest assets. The UI should recede and let them breathe.
3. **No friction between listening and buying** — the path from "I like this" to "I own this" (merch, stream) should be immediate and obvious.
4. **Mobile-first, always** — the core audience discovers on phones. Every layout decision starts there.
5. **Earned moments** — don't animate for animation's sake. Motion should feel like something is arriving, not performing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Touch targets 44px minimum on mobile. Reduced motion respect via prefers-reduced-motion.
